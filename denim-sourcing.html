Denim sourcing

Denim, from fabric choice to final wash.

We source denim fabrics and coordinate the development of jeans and denim garments, including the washes and finishes that define the final product.

Denim fabric options

The right denim for the right fit.

Weight, weave, stretch content and finish decide how a garment looks, wears and ages. We help you choose with the end customer and price point in mind.

  • Raw denim. Unwashed, deep indigo, for brands that want fades to develop with wear.
  • Washed denim. Pre-finished for softness, colour and consistent shade.
  • Rigid denim. Structured, classic hand-feel.
  • Stretch denim. Comfort and recovery with varying elastane levels.
  • Selvedge denim. Available subject to verified capability.

Shorts & skirts

Washes & finishes

Where denim gets its character.

Wash and finish choices change appearance, hand-feel and cost. We coordinate sampling against your reference so shade and fade are agreed before bulk production.

  • Rinse
  • Stone and enzyme wash
  • Vintage looks
  • Whiskering & hand finishes
  • Coatings & overdyes

Available finishes depend on the manufacturing partner selected for each project.
