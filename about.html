About

A sourcing partner built around two categories.

We help clothing brands, retailers and wholesalers turn a concept into a finished, production-ready garment, with knitwear and denim at the centre of what we do.

Our approach

Specification first. Then the supply chain.

Many brands begin by looking for a factory. We begin by understanding the garment: the fabric, the construction, the finish, the price point and the customer. Only then do we match the right materials and manufacturing partners to the brief.

That sequence reduces rework in sampling, avoids mismatched expectations and gives you a production standard that is documented, not assumed.

Philosophy

Fewer categories, clearer accountability.

Depth over breadth

We focus on knitwear and denim so our fabric and construction knowledge stays sharp.

Transparency

Costs, timelines and risks are explained plainly, including when the answer is not what you hoped.

Commercial sense

Great product that cannot be made at the right cost is not a good product. We design with margin in mind.

How we work with buyers

A relationship, not a transaction.

You get a single contact who understands your brief and your commercial goals. We consolidate feedback, keep sampling moving and report honestly on progress.

For startups, that means practical guidance on minimums, fabric choices and development sequencing. For established brands, it means a dependable extension of your product development and sourcing team.
