End-to-end process

One thread from brief to shipment.

Most sourcing problems come from handoffs between fabric, sampling and production. We keep one line of ownership across all three.

  1. Brief & specification

    We review your references, tech pack, target price and quantities, and flag risks early.

  2. Fabric & material selection

    Fabric options matched to hand-feel, weight, performance and cost, with swatches for approval.

  3. Development & sampling

    Pattern, fit and prototype rounds coordinated with comments consolidated into clear revisions.

  4. Production coordination

    Approved samples become the production standard. We track milestones and keep you informed.

  5. Quality, finishing & packing

    Inspection at defined stages, garment finishing, labelling and packaging to your requirements.

  6. Shipment coordination

    Export documentation and logistics coordination where supported for your destination.

Product development & sampling

Concept to approved sample, without the guesswork.

Whether you arrive with a full tech pack or a reference garment and a mood board, we translate intent into specification: fabric, construction, measurements, trims, washes.
