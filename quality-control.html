Quality control

Quality checked at the stages that matter.

We build inspection into the workflow so issues are found early, documented clearly and resolved before they reach your warehouse.

  1. Material checks

    Incoming fabric and trims are checked against approved swatches, specification and shade.

  2. Pre-production review

    Pre-production samples are compared with the written specification before cutting starts.

  3. In-line inspection

    Checks during sewing and finishing to catch construction and measurement issues early.

  4. Final inspection

    Finished goods checked against the approved sample, measurement tolerances and packing instructions.

  5. Reporting

    Findings summarised in clear reports so decisions are quick and informed.

Testing coordination

Fabric testing, arranged for you.

Where your brief requires lab testing, such as shrinkage, colour fastness or composition, we coordinate sampling to accredited labs of your choosing or on your behalf, and share the results.

Certifications and standards are stated only where verified. Ask us which apply to your project.
