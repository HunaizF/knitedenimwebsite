Knitwear sourcing

Knit fabrics and garments, specified with care.

From jersey and fleece to finished T-shirts, polos and hoodies, we source the right knit for the garment and manage development through to production.

Knit fabric categories

Choose the structure, then the yarn and finish.

Knit performance depends on structure, yarn, GSM and finishing. We help you balance hand-feel, shape retention, shrinkage and cost.

  • Single jersey. Lightweight and versatile for tees and base layers.
  • Interlock. Smoother, more stable double-knit for premium basics.
  • Rib. Stretch and recovery for trims, tops and fitted styles.
  • Pique. Textured surface for polo shirts and sportswear.
  • Fleece & French terry. Looped or brushed backs for hoodies, sweatshirts and joggers.
  • Cotton and blended knits. Fibre blends tuned for comfort, durability and price.

Organic cotton knitwear is offered subject to verified capability and certification at the time of enquiry.
