Product development

From concept to approved sample.

A clear development workflow that turns references, sketches and tech packs into production-ready garments.

  1. Concept & references

    We review sketches, reference garments, mood boards and target price, then confirm the development scope.

  2. Tech pack coordination

    We help complete or refine your tech pack: measurements, construction, trims, labels, washes and packing.

  3. Fabric & trim selection

    Fabric options, swatches and lab-dips for approval before sampling.

  4. Pattern & first sample

    Patterns made to specification and first prototypes produced for review.

  5. Fit development

    Measured fit comments consolidated into clear revisions, with as many rounds as the garment needs.

  6. Pre-production sample

    The approved sample and written specification become the production standard.

Product specifications

What a good brief contains.

  • Garment type, fit and size range
  • Fabric composition, weight and finish
  • Construction and stitching detail
  • Trims, labels and packaging
  • Target price and quantities
  • Timeline and destination market
